Privacy Policy for ToDoTree

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

1. Core Principle: Offline-First & Local Storage

ToDoTree operates primarily offline. All tasks, hierarchies, checklists, notes, and attachments are stored locally on your device using a private SQLite database. We do not sell your personal data or transmit it to external tracking or advertising servers.

2. Permissions Used

  • Location: Used only when you manually pin your GPS location to a task. Never tracked in the background.
  • Audio & Camera: Used solely to capture voice memos or images/videos you attach to tasks.
  • Contacts: Used only when linking contact quick-actions (Call/SMS/WhatsApp) to a task.
  • Calendar: Used only to synchronize scheduled tasks with your selected Google Calendar.
  • Notifications & Alarms: Used to trigger alarms for reminders, due dates, and repeat intervals.

3. Data Deletion

All data is under your control. Deleting a task in the app or uninstalling ToDoTree removes all data from your device.
